GWS Access

GWS Access is a personal command-line tool (Google Workspace CLI) operated by Daniel Sadilek for his own Google account only. It is not offered to other users.

Privacy policy

The tool accesses Google Workspace data (Drive, Docs, Sheets, Gmail, Calendar) solely on behalf of the account owner, on the owner's own computer. No data is transmitted to, stored by, or shared with any third party. OAuth tokens are stored locally in the operating system keyring. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
